## Dota 2 hero imagery

Hero imagery is downloaded from Valve's Dota 2 web CDN.

Portrait pattern:

`https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/apps/dota2/images/dota_react/heroes/{slug}.png`

Full portraits are locally encoded to WebP for delivery.

Icon pattern:

`https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/apps/dota2/images/dota_react/heroes/icons/{slug}.png`

Used slugs:

- `antimage`
- `axe`
- `crystal_maiden`
- `disruptor`
- `juggernaut`
- `lina`
- `phantom_assassin`
- `puck`
- `pudge`
- `shadow_shaman`
- `sven`
- `viper`
